## Fact check (sources)
- Kalinga war ≈ 261 BCE, ~8th year of Ashoka's reign — Ashoka's **Rock Edict XIII**.
- Edict XIII: 150,000 people deported, 100,000 killed, "many times that number" perished — Ashoka's own words.
- Edict XIII expresses his remorse; he turns to *dhamma* (righteous conduct).
- Edicts carved on rocks and pillars across the empire; Lion Capital of Sarnath (pillar) is India's national emblem; the 24-spoke Ashoka Chakra is on India's flag.
- Sent Buddhist missions abroad (incl. his son Mahinda to Sri Lanka — Sri Lankan chronicles).
- Earlier life details (e.g. "Chandashoka", killing 99 brothers) are **legends** from later Buddhist texts → stated as legend or avoided.
- Edicts were forgotten; script deciphered by **James Prinsep in 1837**.

## Character reference (reuse in every prompt)
ASHOKA: Indian emperor, early 30s, strong jaw, thin moustache, dark wavy shoulder-length hair, gold crown with a single red gem, deep maroon and gold armour, white sash; later scenes: same face, saffron-white simple robe, no crown.
Style suffix for every image prompt: *painterly cinematic 2D cartoon, Disney-Pixar concept art meets Arcane, dramatic teal night palette with orange firelight, volumetric smoke and embers, vertical 9:16 composition, no text*.
